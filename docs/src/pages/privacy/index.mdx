# Privacy

Dusklapse runs entirely on your device and on your camera. It has no server, no account and no
analytics. Nothing you photograph, and nothing about where or when you photograph it, is sent
anywhere.

_Last updated: 4 September 2026_

## What the app does with your data

**Your photographs stay yours.** Your camera writes its files to its own memory card. To show you
a preview and to measure how bright the frame is, Dusklapse asks the camera for a small JPEG copy
and holds it on your device for as long as it is on screen. That copy is never uploaded, never
stored in a photo library, and never shared. The originals never leave the camera.

**Your location is used and immediately discarded.** The optional "Auto Luminance" feature works
out how far the sun sits below the horizon, which needs a rough position and the time. That
calculation happens on your device, the result is a single number, and the position itself is
neither stored nor transmitted. The feature is optional: without it the app never asks for your
location, and declining the permission leaves everything else working.

**The local network permission is for your camera.** iOS asks for it because Dusklapse connects
directly to the camera's Wi-Fi. It is used for nothing else, and the app makes no connections to
the internet at all.

## What is stored on your device

- The camera you last connected to: manufacturer, address and port, so the app can offer it again.
- Your settings: exposure limits, the daylight curve, how often frames are transferred.
- A log of what the app and the camera did, which the log view in the app shows you. It stays on
  the device and is only shared if you choose to send it to us with a bug report.

All of it lives in the app's own storage and is removed when you delete the app.

## What we do not do

There is no tracking, no advertising, no profiling and no third-party analytics. Dusklapse
contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not sell or share data, because we do not have
any.

## What Apple sees

Apple provides us with aggregated, anonymised crash reports and usage statistics for apps
downloaded from the App Store, and with crash data and usage statistics for builds distributed
through TestFlight. Whether that data is shared is controlled by your own iOS settings, under
**Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements**. We cannot identify individual
people from it. This processing is Apple's; see
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## This website

The documentation site you are reading is hosted by Vercel, which records standard server logs
such as IP addresses and requested pages in order to deliver it. The site sets no tracking
cookies.

## Your rights

Because the app processes no personal data on our side, there is normally nothing for us to
disclose, correct or delete. If you have written to us — a bug report, an email, a GitHub issue —
you can ask what we hold, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and complain to a supervisory
authority. Under the GDPR the German authorities are listed by the
[Federal Commissioner for Data Protection](https://www.bfdi.bund.de/).
